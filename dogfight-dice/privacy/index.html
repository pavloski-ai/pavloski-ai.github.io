Dogfight Dice – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Dogfight Dice (package com.pavloskiai.dogfightdice), made by Pavloski.AI, uses and why. In short: we don't run any servers of our own, we don't ask for accounts, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Ads, online play and the in-app purchase use services from Google and Unity, which process some data as described below.

Information kept on your phone

The game saves a few things on your device so they are there the next time you play:

  • your sound setting;
  • the player names you type in;
  • how many games you've finished and when the last ad was shown, so ads stay infrequent;
  • whether you bought “Remove ads”.

This information stays on your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Online play

When you host or join an online game, the game uses Unity Gaming Services by Unity Technologies:

  • Authentication signs you in anonymously. Unity creates a random player ID; no name, email or account is involved.
  • Relay passes game messages between the two phones. To do this Unity's servers process your device's IP address. The player name you entered and your moves are sent to the other player in that game.

Same-phone games and games against the computer don't use these services. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Ads

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob: none in your first session, then at most one after every few finished games, never during a game. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalised.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change it at any time with Privacy settings in the game's menu.
  • On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall. Buying it turns off all ads in the game.

Other technical data

The game is made with the Unity engine, which may collect limited technical information such as device model and operating system version, as described in Unity's privacy policy linked above. Google Play may also provide us with aggregated crash and performance statistics, which do not identify you.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information and we don't share it with anyone beyond the services named above, which process it to provide online play, ads and purchases.

Children

Dogfight Dice is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Uninstall the game, or clear its data, to delete everything stored on your phone.
  • Use Privacy settings in the game (where shown) and your phone's ad settings to control ads.
  • Since we don't hold your data ourselves, requests about data processed by Google or Unity are handled under their policies linked above. We're happy to help: email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
